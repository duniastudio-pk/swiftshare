This policy informs you of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application and the choices you have associated with that data.

By installing, accessing, or using SwiftShare, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Application.

2. Architecture & Data Philosophy

SwiftShare was engineered from the ground up to empower users to transfer files and communicate freely without relying on cloud infrastructure:

  • Peer-to-Peer Transmission: Transfers, chat messaging, and calls operate directly between devices over local sockets (TCP/UDP), local Wi-Fi Direct, or mobile hotspot.
  • Zero Cloud Intermediaries: We do not route your files, messages, or voice/video streams through any cloud relay, remote server, or intermediate database.
  • Local AES-256 Encryption: Chat messages, voice notes, and signaling between peers are encrypted end-to-end locally using military-grade AES-256 encryption before transmission over the local network.
  • Isolated Web Share: Direct Web Share allows cross-platform browser transfers (iPhone, Mac, PC, Linux) via an isolated local HTTP server on port 8080 with optional session PIN protection.

3. Information We Collect & Process

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile Display Name & Avatar: When you set up your profile, your chosen display name and avatar image are saved locally on your device's private storage (Room database). When your device is discoverable on a local network, your name and avatar are broadcast strictly to nearby peers via local mDNS/UDP so they can recognize your device in the radar. This information is never uploaded to any external server.
  • Shared Files & Media: Any photos, videos, music, documents, or apps you select for transfer remain on your device until sent directly to your chosen peer. SwiftShare never uploads, scans, or analyzes your files.
  • Chat Messages & Voice Notes: Text messages, voice recordings, and media exchanged within direct or group chats are stored solely in your local device database. You can clear chat history or delete messages at any time directly within the app.
  • P2P Audio & Video Calls: Camera and microphone streams during peer-to-peer calls are streamed directly between the two connected devices over the local network and are never recorded or uploaded to any server.

B. Information Automatically Processed Locally

  • Local Network IP Addresses: To establish direct socket connections, SwiftShare identifies your device's assigned local IP address (e.g., 192.168.x.x). This address is used solely for local socket communication and is never transmitted over the internet to us.
  • Device Model & Type: Your device model (e.g., Phone, Tablet, PC) may be shared locally with peers to display an appropriate icon in the nearby devices radar.
  • Installed Applications (Local APK Sharing): When you open the Apps tab to share installed applications, SwiftShare queries your device's installed packages locally to display them in the picker. This information is used strictly on-device to let you extract and transfer APK files to your peers. SwiftShare never tracks, logs, or transmits your list of installed apps to any external server.

C. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT require user account creation, registration, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • We do NOT access your contact list or address book.
  • We do NOT track or collect your physical GPS location.
  • We do NOT employ third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer) to track your behavior or app usage.

SwiftShare integrates Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements that support the ongoing maintenance and development of this free application.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), device information, and approximate location data (derived from IP address) to serve relevant ads according to Google's advertising policies.

  • Google AdMob is the only external third-party SDK in SwiftShare that communicates over the internet.
  • Your transferred files, chat messages, voice notes, and P2P communication are completely isolated from and never accessible to Google AdMob.
  • For details on how Google processes user data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

6. Data Retention & User Rights (Data Deletion)

SwiftShare complies fully with Google Play's User Data and Data Deletion policies:

  • No User Accounts Required: SwiftShare does not require account creation, login, or profile registration with any remote service. Because no user account or personal data is stored on remote servers, no account deletion request is necessary.
  • No Cloud Retention: DUNIA Studio does not operate cloud servers and does not retain, store, or possess any copies of your files, chat transcripts, or media.
  • Complete User Autonomy: All data resides exclusively on your local device. You have complete autonomy to delete chat messages, clear transfer history, or delete received files from your device storage at any time directly through the app interface.
  • Immediate Local Deletion: Clearing the app's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the Application permanently deletes all locally stored databases, chat logs, profile settings, and temporary files immediately.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SwiftShare is a general-utility productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

8. Security Safeguards

We take data security seriously:

  • Chat messages and signaling transmitted between peer devices over local networks are encrypted using AES-256.
  • Direct Web Share operates over an isolated local HTTP server with customizable session PIN authentication to prevent unauthorized local network access.
  • Hotspot sessions generate randomized WPA2/WPA3 passphrases.
  • Transfers occur entirely within your local network boundary and never leave your local Wi-Fi or hotspot.
